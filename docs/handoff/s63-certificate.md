## Done

- Claimed: issue #112, this file, a draft PR.
- Source found: the official text of the BSA, 2023 (Act 47 of 2023) on India Code, `https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20063/1/aa202347.pdf`, "As on the 6th October, 2025". Section 63 is on pp. 30-31; THE SCHEDULE [See section 63(4)(c)], CERTIFICATE, Part A (to be filled by the Party) and Part B (to be filled by the Expert), on pp. 52-53.

## Decisions

- The certificate's wording is copied from that PDF by a script (`backend/scripts/fetch_bsa_schedule.py`, run by hand), with the URL, the "as on" date and the PDF's SHA-256 written beside it. Never typed from memory (rule 7); regenerated by the script, never edited by hand.
- Always a draft: never "certified" or "admissible" (rule 11); it says it is not valid until signed.
- Agreed with Barenyam (13 Nov): Part B is left blank for the expert; the device particulars are typed when drafting and kept nowhere; the draft is a PDF from the start.
- Two readings must agree before the wording is written: pdftotext (spacing kept) and pypdf, compared without spaces. pypdf alone splits "electro nic" on p. 52.
- IBM Plex has no □, so the PDF draws each tick box as a square, with a ✓ (which Plex has) when ticked.
